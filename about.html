About Me

Welcome to my personal website. I am an applied microeconomist with a concentration in the energy sector, with particular interest in nuclear fission. My research spans the fields of political economy, regulatory economics, and industrial organization. In my day job at Kairos Power, my work consists of financial modeling, forecasting costs and schedule, and generating strategic insights for the company.
